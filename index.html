How to Play

Your mission is simple: walk around, greet classmates, ask simple questions in English, and find people who match the boxes. Write the person's name in the box. Complete 4 lines to win a small gift from your teacher!

① Mingle
Walk around and meet different classmates.
② Ask
Use English questions to find a match.
③ Write
Write the student's name in the matching box.
④ Bingo!
Complete 4 horizontal, vertical, or diagonal lines.
